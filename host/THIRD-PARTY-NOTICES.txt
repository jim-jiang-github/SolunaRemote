The upstream binary may contain statically linked Qt tray support.
SolunaRemote disables the Sunshine tray and does not invoke its installer,
service registration, driver installation or independent user interface.
The soluna-stream client uses moonlight-common-c, FFmpeg and SDL directly,
without requiring a separately installed client application.
